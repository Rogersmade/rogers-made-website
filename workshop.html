The story

The dream was always to build a small cabin-style workshop. Somewhere big enough to turn a hobby into something more.

A place where I could spend my time doing what I love: creating, crafting and working with wood.

What started as a hobby has slowly grown into a small creative business.

This isn't about mass production or making things by the hundred. It's about enjoying the process, taking pride in the details and creating something special.

Today, I'm sharing that love and passion through unique, personalised products and carefully restored and upcycled furniture.

Every piece has a beginning, a process and a story of its own.
