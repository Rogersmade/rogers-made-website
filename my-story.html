My Story

Where it began

A lifetime of making,
creating and learning.

I've always been someone who enjoys making things. Over the years, my work has taken me in several different directions, from carpentry and joinery to bespoke landscaping in the Cotswolds, garden projects and hard surface restoration.

Each chapter has taught me something different, given me new skills and, most importantly, allowed me to do something I genuinely enjoy – working with my hands and turning ideas into something real.

Carpentry, joinery and restoration

Learning the craft

I've spent years working with different materials, learning new techniques and taking pride in the finished result.

Whether it's building something from scratch, transforming an outdoor space or carefully restoring a damaged surface, I've always enjoyed the process just as much as the end result.

A new chapter

Rogers Made.

Rogers Made is a chance to bring all those experiences together and do something a little different.

What started as a love of making and experimenting has grown into my own little workshop, where I can explore new ideas, work with beautiful materials and create things that have a little more meaning.

I particularly enjoy working with solid oak, combining traditional woodworking with modern techniques such as laser engraving and printing to create something unique.

I'm not interested in mass production. I want to make things that people connect with, whether it's a personalised gift, a special keepsake or something beautiful for their home.

For me, it's about the little details, the satisfaction of making something by hand and knowing that something I've created might become part of somebody else's special moment.

Rogers Made is more than just a business. It's the next chapter of something I've always loved doing.
