PUT YOUR PRODUCT PHOTOS IN THIS FOLDER
--------------------------------------

1. Name each photo simply: lowercase, dashes instead of spaces, no
   special characters. For example:
     oak-table-lamp-1.jpg
     oak-table-lamp-2.jpg

2. Upload the photos into this folder (assets/images/products/).

3. Open assets/js/products.js and list the photos in the product's
   "images", starting each one with /assets/images/products/ :

     images: [
       "/assets/images/products/oak-table-lamp-1.jpg",
       "/assets/images/products/oak-table-lamp-2.jpg"
     ],

   The first photo is the main one on the card. Any extra photos show
   as thumbnails on the product page. Use up to FIVE photos per
   product (any more are ignored).

Photos straight from your phone are fine. The site automatically resizes
and compresses them for visitors. Landscape or square photos look best
on the product cards. .jpg, .png and .webp files all work.
