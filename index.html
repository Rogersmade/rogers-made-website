Inside the workshop

Slow, careful work.
One piece at a time.

Every Rogers Made piece starts and finishes in the same place: my own workshop in the UK. There's no factory line and no outsourcing. Each design is drawn, cut, shaped and finished by me.

Around 90% of the timber I use is hardwood, including oak. I choose it for its strength, its weight in the hand and the grain that makes every board different from the last.

01 — Select

Choosing the timber

Each board is picked for its grain, colour and character, with hardwoods like oak making up most of what I work with.

02 — Cut

Marking & cutting

Designs are measured and marked out by hand, then cut to size, working with the grain rather than against it.

03 — Shape

Shaping & detail

Edges, curves, lettering and personal details are shaped and refined until the piece feels right in the hand.

04 — Finish

Sanding & finishing

Each surface is sanded and finished by hand to protect the wood and bring the natural grain forward.

Behind Rogers Made

A little workshop.
A lot of imagination.

Rogers Made started with a love of making, experimenting and turning ideas into something real.

Working with timber, creating designs, cutting, shaping, finishing and bringing each piece to life — everything is made with care in my own workshop.

I believe wood should be celebrated. Its grain, character and imperfections are part of what makes every piece unique. That's why nothing here is mass-produced. Every item is designed and made by me, one at a time.
